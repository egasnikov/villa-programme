What we do

A disciplined fix-and-flip operator, now focused on larger villas

Palm & Peak has completed eight value-add projects in Dubai since 2024: seven waterfront apartments and one villa. The same method now moves up in size, where the gap between a dated house on a great plot and a finished one is measured in tens of millions.

The business is simple to describe. We source villas in established, low-supply communities where the plot and the position are the value and the house is not. We buy below asking, against registered comparable sales. We then rebuild the interior, extend the built-up area to the permitted limit, and re-landscape, working to a fixed scope agreed before the purchase closes. The finished villa is sold to an end user through a broker network that Palm & Peak has built and manages directly.

Each project sits in its own SPV. The investor funds the equity in full; Palm & Peak underwrites, executes and sells, and is paid principally from profit above a preferred return to the investor. There is no debt in the base case, no cross-collateralisation between projects, and no exit that depends on the market moving.

Why bigger villas, why now

The spread between a dated villa and a finished one is the return

In Al Barari and Jumeirah Golf Estates the land is scarce and the buyer is an end user. Renovated villas have printed AED 4,800 to 5,300 per square foot; unrenovated stock on the same streets trades at AED 3,500 to 3,750. The work in between is what we do.

Scarce land, dated houses

The first generation of Dubai's gated villa communities is 15 to 20 years old. Plots of 10,000 to 15,000 sqft on golf or garden frontage cannot be created again, but most of the houses on them were finished to a 2008 developer standard.

End users pay for finished

The buyer of a AED 40M to 80M villa is a family relocating or upgrading, not a trader. They pay a premium to avoid an 18-month build, and the premium is visible in registered prices: renovated resales at Jasmine Leaf 5 set the AED 80M benchmark in July 2025 and the developer buyer of an unrenovated Desert Leaf 3 paid AED 3,573 per sqft four months later.

Fewer competitors, larger profit per deal

Apartment flipping is crowded and the ticket is small. A villa redevelopment ties up capital for 12 to 18 months but returns AED 8M to 17M of profit per project against AED 0.7M to 1.8M on an apartment, with the same team and the same method.

How we execute

One method, run by the principals on every project

The process is the same on a AED 6M apartment and a AED 60M villa. What changes is the size of the numbers and the length of the build.

1

Source off market

Direct owner approaches and a broker network of 600+ agents managed in our CRM. We look at many villas to buy one.

2

Underwrite to registered comps

Exit price anchored to Dubai Land Department transactions of renovated villas on the same street, never to asking prices. Sensitivity tested before an offer goes in.

3

Buy below asking

Offers are made against the evidence, with walk-away discipline. Al Barari Type B is negotiated at AED 38.0M against a AED 40.0M ask.

4

Redevelop and extend

Fixed-scope, fixed-price contracts with staged draws and a held contingency. Extension to the GFA cap, full interior rebuild, landscape, staging.

5

Sell turnkey through brokers

Listing at the top of the renovated set, distributed through our broker network with professional photography and a furnished, finished house.

Risks and mitigants

What can go wrong, and what we do about it

Exit price below target

Every target is anchored below or level with registered renovated sales on the same street, never to listings. Sensitivity is published for each project: Al Barari stays profitable to a AED 65M exit, JGE to AED 37M. Any sale below target needs the investor's approval.

Construction cost overrun

Scope is specified and priced before the purchase closes. Fixed-price contracts, staged draws against progress, a held contingency, and Vadim Kniazev on site. Overruns above 5% of contingency go to the investor for approval.

Time to sell

Trophy villas in low-supply communities have a small pool of buyers but little competing stock. The sensitivity grids show returns across 12 to 24 months. If no offers arrive within 60 days of listing, a pre-agreed price adjustment protocol applies; the villa can also be let while marketed.

Extension and GFA approval

Extensions are sized to the permitted GFA, priced at market rates per sqft, and the base exit does not depend on approvals beyond the cap. Design and approvals run in parallel with the purchase.

Market softening

Entry below asking and below competing bids, an end-user buyer base, and no debt: a softer market lengthens the hold rather than forcing a sale. Dubai villa prices rose 17% year on year in H1 2026 on DLD data.

Key-person and operator risk

Two principals run every project directly with a lean team, which keeps overhead low but concentrates execution. Investor rights in the SPV (approvals, account access, step-in on default) sit alongside contractor agreements held by the SPV, not by the operator.
